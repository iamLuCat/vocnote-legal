Privacy Policy for VocNote

Last updated: September 30, 2026

VocNote (“the app”, “we”, “us”) is a vocabulary notebook for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and the choices you have. We designed VocNote to keep your notebook on your device by default and to collect as little as possible.

1. Your vocabulary content

The words, meanings, notes, examples and other content you add stay on your device. They are uploaded to our cloud only if you create a VocNote Cloud account, sign in, and turn on cloud sync (a Premium feature). If you never sign in, none of your vocabulary content leaves your device through us. You can export and import your data yourself at any time (JSON/CSV).

2. Account information

If you choose to use VocNote Cloud, we create an account using Firebase Authentication (by Google). You can sign up with an email and password, Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. We store your email address, an optional display name, an optional profile photo, and a unique account identifier so you can sign in and sync across your devices. If you use Sign in with Apple with “Hide My Email”, we only receive Apple’s relay address. Using an account is entirely optional — the app is fully usable without one.

3. Cloud sync data

When cloud sync is enabled, your vocabulary content and learning progress are stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore so they can sync between your devices. This data is tied to your account and is not shared with other users. Your profile photo, if you add one, is stored in Firebase Cloud Storage.

3a. Features you choose to use

Each of these is off until you use it, and each sends only what is described:

  • Leaderboard (opt-in). If you join it, your display name, profile photo, current streak and number of mastered words are visible to other VocNote users. Leaving the leaderboard removes your entry.
  • Push notifications (opt-in). If you allow them, we store a device token (Firebase Cloud Messaging) with your account to tell you about new vocabulary sets or replies to your feedback. Signing out removes this device’s token. The daily study reminder is scheduled on your device and sends nothing.
  • Pictures on Google Drive. If you connect Google Drive, word pictures are saved in the app’s private folder in your own Drive (the drive.appdata permission, which cannot see any of your other files). We do not receive a copy.
  • Import from Google Sheets. The link you paste is used to read that sheet through the Google Sheets API. Nothing is written back.
  • Feedback and suggestions. Messages you send us, and suggestions for vocabulary maps, are stored with your account identifier and name so we can reply and, if you choose, credit you.
  • Invite friends. To count an invitation once, we store which account invited which, and a one-way hash of the invited account’s email address (not the address itself).

Apple Intelligence suggestions, pronunciation and handwriting practice run on your device; nothing you type into them is sent to us. Some content (such as Chinese stroke-order data) is downloaded from a public content network, which, like any website, sees your IP address.

4. Usage and analytics

We use Firebase Analytics (by Google) to understand how the app is used (for example, which features are opened) so we can improve it. This analytics data includes app-interaction events, general device and OS information, and a pseudonymous identifier. It does not include the content of your notebook (your words, meanings or notes are never sent as analytics). Advertising identifiers (IDFA) are not used, and we do not use this data to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

5. Crash and diagnostic reports

Crash reporting via Firebase Crashlytics (by Google) is turned off by default. It only runs if you enable it in Settings › Diagnostics. When enabled, it collects crash logs and diagnostic information (such as the state of the app at the time of a crash and device information) to help us fix problems. You can turn it off again at any time.

6. Advertising

The free version of VocNote shows ads through Google AdMob. We request non-personalized ads only. AdMob may process device information and an advertising identifier to serve ads, cap how often you see them, and prevent fraud, but we do not use them to build an advertising profile of you or to track you across other apps, and the app does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt. Ad attribution is handled through Apple’s privacy-preserving SKAdNetwork. Buying VocNote Premium removes all ads.

7. Purchases

Purchases of VocNote Premium are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Payment is handled entirely by them — we never receive your card or payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage entitlements and to let you restore your purchase. RevenueCat processes your purchase status, a purchase/customer identifier, device information, and — if you are signed in to VocNote Cloud — your account identifier and email, so your purchase can be linked to your account and restored on your devices.

7a. App integrity and updates

We use Firebase App Check (Apple DeviceCheck / Google Play Integrity) to confirm requests come from a genuine copy of the app, and Firebase Remote Config to tell you when an update is required. These process device and app information, not your notebook.

7b. VocNote on social media

We publish short videos about English words to VocNote’s own accounts on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. To do this, our publishing tool connects to those platforms with our own account’s permission (for example, a Facebook Page access token) and only uploads videos we made. It does not read, collect or store any information about people who watch, like, comment on or follow those accounts. What you do on those platforms is covered by their own privacy policies.

8. Third-party services

The services above are provided by third parties who process data on our behalf. Their own privacy policies apply:

  • Google (AdMob, Firebase, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Play): policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat: revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple: apple.com/legal/privacy

9. How we share data

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with anyone except the service providers listed above, strictly to operate the app, or where required by law.

10. Data retention and deletion

  • On-device data: removed when you delete the app from your device.
  • Cloud account and synced data: kept while your account exists. You can delete your account and all of its cloud data at any time from inside the app — open Settings and tap Delete account at the foot of your profile. This deletes your synced notebook, profile photo, leaderboard entry, device tokens, feedback, suggestions and invitation records. The only thing kept is the one-way email hash described above, so an address cannot claim the invitation reward twice; it cannot be turned back into your email.
  • Pictures on Google Drive stay in your Drive until you remove them there (Google Drive › Settings › Manage apps › VocNote › Delete hidden app data).
  • By email: write to lucat.developer@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete your account and data within 30 days.

11. Your choices

  • Use the app without creating an account.
  • Turn crash reporting on or off in Settings.
  • Join or leave the leaderboard, and allow or refuse notifications.
  • Remove all ads by purchasing VocNote Premium.
  • Export your data at any time (JSON/CSV).

12. Children

VocNote is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Data security and international transfers

We rely on the security measures of the service providers above. Because those providers (Google, RevenueCat, Apple) operate globally, data may be processed on servers outside your country.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top reflects the latest version.

15. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or want your data deleted, contact us at: lucat.developer@gmail.com
